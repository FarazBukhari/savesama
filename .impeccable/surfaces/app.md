## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a tape deck for the feed: one yellow label block, a red REC dot on Save, and a three-span tape line (Server · To phone · Saved) that says where the file is. It refuses the downloader default of near-black with one neon or orange accent and stacked cards.
OWN-WORLD: Deck yellow #FFD12E fields and selections, ink-black actions, neutral ground #F3F3EF / #111110, tone steps instead of lines, Michroma wide titles, Schibsted Grotesk text with tabular figures, Phosphor regular icons.
STORY: Share or paste a link, check the preview, tap the REC Save, watch the tape fill to Saved, find it in Library.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Grab: full-bleed yellow deck holding wordmark, server pill, paste well with Paste/Get, clipboard suggestion; below, Now saving job cards with tape lines; Material nav bar with yellow indicator.
